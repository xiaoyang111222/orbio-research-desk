# Robinhood Chain fees slide 69% from peak as Nasdaq bets $100M on tokenised equity pivot

Robinhood Chain daily revenue fell to $1.73M on 11 Sep from a $5.60M peak on 4 Sep, down 69% per DefiLlama, even as DEX volume remains elevated at $1.5–2.7B/day and stablecoin supply crossed $1B. Nasdaq Ventures invested $100M in Kraken parent Payward to launch Nasdaq Equity Tokens with full shareholder rights by Q2 2027, contrasting with existing wrapper products: Kraken's xStocks reached $800M backing and 300k holders across 715 instruments (economic exposure only), while Robinhood Stock Tokens offer economic claims without voting. Orbio credit marketplace shows $27k liquidity across discount tiers; token at $0.0185, down 7.2% in 24h, with $3.36M volume.
